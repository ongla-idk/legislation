About

How legislation and parliamentary business move through Parliament.

This site is a public record of legislation and parliamentary business in the Blue Republic. It publishes what Parliament has done and is doing — it is not the system Parliament itself uses to conduct its business.

How a Bill becomes law

A Bill is introduced, given a first reading, examined at committee stage, and put to a final vote. If it passes, it goes to the President for assent. Once assented, it commences either immediately or on a date set by order, and becomes an Act.

Document statuses

Bills move through statuses from Draft through to Enacted, Rejected or Withdrawn. Enacted laws are marked In force, Partially in force, Not yet in force, Amended, Suspended, Repealed or Expired, so it's always clear whether a law currently has effect.

Parliamentary business

Motions and resolutions follow a simpler path: Tabled, Scheduled, Under debate, and then Passed, Rejected, Withdrawn or Superseded.

National Energy Security Act 2026

ACT-2026-009 · Formal citation: 2026 c. 9
Date introduced
12 Mar 2026
Sponsor
Minister for State Administration
Responsible ministry
State Administration
Presidential assent
28 Jun 2026

Summary

This Act establishes a national framework for securing the Blue Republic's energy supply, creates the Office of Energy Security to monitor reserves and infrastructure, and sets out emergency powers the government may use during a declared supply shortage.

Document text

Part 1 — Preliminary
1. This Act may be cited as the National Energy Security Act 2026.
2. This Act comes into force on the date of presidential assent, save for Part 3, which comes into force on a date appointed by order.

Part 2 — Office of Energy Security
3. There is established an Office of Energy Security, responsible for monitoring national energy reserves and reporting annually to Parliament.
